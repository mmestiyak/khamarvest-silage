## What You Need to Do (One-Time Setup, ~10 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Google My Business Account
1. Go to **google.com/business** (or Google Map Search)
2. Click "Manage your business on Google"
3. Sign in with your Google account (create one if needed)
4. Click **"Create Business"**

### Step 2: Fill in Basic Info
- **Business Name**: খামারভেস্ট সাইলেজ (or Khamarvest Silage)
- **Category**: Choose "Agricultural Services" or "Wholesaler"
- **Phone**: +880 1303-438063
- **Website**: https://silage.khamarvest.com
- **Address**: Leave blank if you're delivery-only (WhatsApp-based ordering) OR enter your physical location if you have one

### Step 3: Verify Your Business
Google will ask you to verify in one of these ways:
- **PostCard method** (6-10 days): Google mails a code; you enter it
- **Phone call** (instant): Google calls your number, gives you a code
- **Email** (instant): Google emails a verification link

**Choose phone call** — fastest.

1. In GMB dashboard, go to **"Info"** tab

   - ঢাকা (Dhaka)

## Why This Matters for Local Ranking

When a farmer searches **"সাইলেজ বগুড়ায়"** or **"silage delivery dhaka"**:
1. Google matches the search location to your GMB service areas
2. My schema tells Google you serve that exact district
3. Your business appears in local search results + Google Maps
4. Each district landing page (/area/silage-bogura, etc.) gets boosted because GMB confirms you serve that area
