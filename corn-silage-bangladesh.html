Khamarvest Silage supplies fresh corn (maize) silage to dairy and beef farms across Bangladesh. Whole corn plants are chopped at the correct maturity stage and naturally fermented in 50 kg airtight bags, so your cattle get green-fodder nutrition all year, including winter and monsoon.

Feeding amounts

Typical daily silage amounts used by our farmer customers, based on published extension guidance:

  • Dairy cows: 15-25 kg per day (commonly 20 kg).
  • Beef fattening cattle: 10-20 kg per day (commonly 15 kg).
  • Goats and sheep: 1-2 kg per day.

Silage replaces green fodder in the ration; straw, concentrate and mineral mix are added as advised by a livestock officer or veterinarian. Animals new to silage should be introduced gradually over about 7 days.

Why farms choose bagged corn silage

  • Same price and quality all year, unlike green grass and straw whose prices spike in winter.
  • Airtight bags are unaffected by monsoon humidity and easy to store without special infrastructure.
  • Palatable fermented feed that cattle take to readily.
  • Simple cost planning: one dairy cow at 20 kg/day costs BDT 200 per day, BDT 6,000 per month.
